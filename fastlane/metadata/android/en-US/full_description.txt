LRCSync fetches synchronized lyrics for the music
currently playing on your Android device.

Features:
- Real-time music detection using Android MediaSession.
- Word-by-word and line-by-line synchronized lyrics.
- Local music library browser.
- Automatic LRC file downloading.
- Batch lyrics downloading.
- Multiple configurable lyrics providers.
- Lyrics card generator.
- Material You interface and AMOLED dark mode.

Supports LRCMux, LRCLIB, and LRC.red.

Requires notification listener access to detect
currently playing music.
